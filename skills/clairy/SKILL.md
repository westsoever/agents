# /clairy

Not a status check. Not a daily brief. This only looks at what was **just said in this conversation** — the last explanation, plan, or wall of text — and rewrites it so it's actually easy to hold onto.

## Step 1 — The tone rules (the law for every output this skill produces)
1. Write in plain, simple language. Use the vocabulary of a smart friend texting.
2. If you must use a technical or industry term, define it instantly in parentheses using a basic analogy.
3. Never use AI clichés like "In today's world," "Let's dive in," "Delve," or "Tapestry."
4. Keep sentences short and active. Break big blocks of text into short lists or tiny paragraphs.
5. Focus on what the user needs to know or do right now, rather than abstract theory.

Follow these exactly — don't paraphrase them into something softer.

## Step 2 — Find what to clarify
Take the assistant's most recent substantive message in this conversation — the explanation, plan, or decision just given. Not the user's message. Not files. Not any daily brief or memory search.

## Step 3 — Strip it down
Pull out only:
- What is actually true or decided
- What needs to happen (if anything)
- Any word a non-expert would have to look up

Cut everything else: reasoning walk-throughs, hedging, "as mentioned above," restated context.

## Step 4 — Rewrite under the tone rules
- Plain words. Texting-a-smart-friend register.
- Any technical/industry term → define it instantly in parentheses with a basic analogy.
- No AI clichés ("In today's world," "Let's dive in," "Delve," "Tapestry," etc).
- Short, active sentences. No walls of text.

## Step 5 — Format for ADHD scanning
- Tiny paragraphs (1-2 sentences max) or short bullet lists — never a dense block.
- One bolded line for the single most important thing, if there is one.
- If there's an action needed, put it last, on its own line, starting with a verb.

## Step 6 — Output
Just the recap. No "Here's a summary:" preamble, no meta-commentary about what you did, no closing "let me know if." Start directly with the content.
